The problem

Big missions do not always come with big teams.

  • Big missions, limited specialist teamsFlexible access to multidisciplinary institutional capabilities.
  • Processes depend on individualsProcess mapping, SOPs, workflows and system strengthening.
  • Data is collected but underusedAssessment, analytics, dashboards and decision support.
  • Fundraising is reactiveSupport in fundraising strategy and documents.
  • Reports and presentations consume programme timeStructured documentation and knowledge products.
  • Technology adds complexitySimple, affordable tools aligned to actual workflows.

How we work

Diagnose. Design. Deliver.

We do not stop at recommendations. We help put them into practice.

01

Diagnose

Understand before solving — organisational assessment, gap analysis, digital and funding readiness review.

02

Design

Build the right solution — strategy, framework, SOP, dashboard, roadmap or communication structure.

03

Deliver

Make it happen — implementation support, reports, proposals, dashboards, systems and documentation.
